The Cabinet of Seals

Tàng Ấn Các — cards get a body (real card faces, a fanned draft) and a home (a persistent collection). The loop: play earns rubbings → a rubbing scratches into a random card → copies stack → same card ×3 combines to the next level → levelled cards return to future drafts heavier. From the master dossier, §05.

01

The loop being built

Three design guards — do not relitigate in code review

1. Ownership never gates the draft pool — all 50 cards stay draftable from run one (thin early drafts killed more roguelite metas than weak metas did). 2. Levels mostly buy draft weight, ~+3–4% effect per level, inside the dossier-§03 power budget (draft honesty). 3. The opening hand charges ambition — +2 per slotted card via the existing AMBITION_PER_POWER_CARD — so meta-power pre-pays the mode's own threat counter. That's the snowball guard.

04

Implementation steps

  1. Declare the card-id freeze. A comment atop ascentCards.ts: ids are append-only save contracts from this commit (exactly like story fragment ids). Renames become migrations. src/data/ascentCards.ts
  2. The store. src/state/cabinet.ts on the legacy.ts pattern: versioned, defensive parse, addCard / addRubbing / spendRubbing / combine / setOpeningHand. new src/state/cabinet.ts
  3. Fill levels[] to 3 for all 50 cards. A content pass: effect step ≈ +30% of the card's own delta per level, display keys updated. i18n .d strings interpolate the display values already — verify each in en and vi. src/data/ascentCards.ts · src/i18n/catalogs/ascent.ts (en + vi)
  4. Draft reads the collection. rollDraft offers a card at its cabinet level and multiplies its weight ×1.3/×1.6. The pool itself is untouched — guard 1. src/systems/ascent/PowerDraftSystem.ts
  5. The card face, baked. src/ui/cardFace.ts: bake frame (rarity colour), motif (the CardIcons vocabulary drawn large), name seal and stars to a RenderTexture, cached per (cardId, level). Every consumer — draft fan, cabinet grid, ceremony — stamps the bake. Never live Graphics per frame; that is the exact cost floor the perf ledger documents. new src/ui/cardFace.ts · pattern: the map bake · RARITY_COLOR in scenes/conquest/constants.ts
  6. The fanned draft. Rewrite showPowerDraft only: hand of 4–5 baked cards fanned across the bottom third, tap to raise, tap again to take; reroll/skip stay in the footer; prompt bus and resolver untouched. Evolution-ready card sits raised by default. src/scenes/conquest/prompts/conquest.ts (showPowerDraft)
  7. Rubbing faucets. +1 in endAscentRun (inside the legacyBanked guard); wave 10/20/30 in the wave director's aftermath; 3–4 launch deeds (first relief march, first evolution, first jade) checked where those events already fire; the Legacy pack purchase on the menu's Legacy page. AscentResolver.ts · wave director · MenuScene.ts (~2664)
  8. The scratch reveal. The thác bản screen: card face under an ink layer that lifts along the pointer. Use RectClip stencils, not masks — Phaser 4 masks are no-ops here (clip-rect-stencil finding), and masks never clip input anyway. Pity counter rendered under the card ("4 pulls since gold"). new src/scenes/conquest/screens/rubbing.ts (or menu-side) · src/ui/RectClip
  9. The cabinet page + combine. Collection grid (owned at level+copies, unfound as dashed silhouettes), combine action with the ×3 ceremony animation, forge row listing learned recipes, opening-hand slots with the ambition price printed on them. new screen beside the dynasty sheet · InkUI conventions (stride by measured cardHeight)
  10. Wire the run. createAscentGameState: apply opening-hand cards at 1 stack each and charge +2 ambition per slot. Ceremony: the Phase-2 bind-card stub becomes real — three cards played this run fan out, chosen one lands as copy-or-new in the cabinet. GameState.ts (createAscentGameState) · prompts/run.ts bind step
  11. Measure. 16-seed funscore vs baseline (opening hand + levels must not move difficulty beyond the ambition price), perf harness at the draft (baked, flat frame cost), /shot review of draft + cabinet + scratch on the phone aspect. /funscore --seeds 16 · /perf · /shot

Traps

  • Masks are stencils: Phaser 4 masks are no-ops for this — the scratch reveal must use RectClip (and the isStencilModifier trap applies); masks also never clip input.
  • InkUI auto-fit: stride lists by measured cardHeight, never a fixed constant — the fan's hit areas especially.
  • The reveal is a reward moment: respect the notification convention — header strip + bell only, no floating toasts over the scratch.
  • Harness autopilot answers prompts: the README-shots round found an autopilot answering "ok" to every new card — pin autopilot behavior before screenshotting drafts.
  • WebGL context exhaustion in long screenshot sessions — reuse one browser context in harnesses.
