# Vạn Thắng Overview

## One-Sentence Pitch

**Vạn Thắng** is a one-hand mobile grand strategy game where the player starts with one castle, drafts heroes, expands across a Vietnam-inspired map, and conquers rival kingdoms through land, politics, and war.

## Core Fantasy

The player begins as a small local lord with:

- 1 castle
- 1 small army
- 1 ruler
- limited food, gold, manpower, stability, and influence

The map is the main progression tree. Every nearby land is a visible strategic desire:

- A farm solves food pressure.
- An iron mountain unlocks stronger infantry.
- A port funds expansion.
- A temple improves ministers and stability.
- An enemy castle is a rival power that must be broken.

## Primary Goal

Capture all rival castles or absorb every rival kingdom.

## Secondary Goals

- Control food supply.
- Build enough economy to pay heroes and armies.
- Recruit heroes that support the current strategy.
- Keep stability high enough to avoid rebellions.
- Capture lands that unlock unit types and stronger actions.
- Destroy rival kingdoms before they outgrow the player.

## Player Experience Target

The game should feel like a compact grand strategy campaign designed for portrait phones. The player should spend most of their time reading the map, dragging across a larger region, tapping lands, comparing tradeoffs, and reacting to real-time pressure.

## First Playable Target

The first playable version should prove:

- The vertical map is readable.
- Touch selection and bottom sheets feel natural.
- Lands are valuable and meaningfully different.
- Real-time progression produces clear consequences.
- The player can acquire, build, recruit, move, battle, and win.
