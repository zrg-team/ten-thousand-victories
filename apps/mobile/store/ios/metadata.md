THE DRUM IS ALREADY FALLING. THEY ARE ALREADY COMING.

One citadel. A handful of spearmen. A country that will not wait for you to be ready.

Take a province — the next invasion comes harder.
Draft a card — the one after that comes harder still.
Everything you win sharpens the blade pointed at you.

There is no victory screen. Only how far you got.

READ THE FIELD OR BLEED FOR IT

Their spears are set. Your line is still forming. The drum falls in five.

Five shapes, each the answer to another — the hedge, the wedge, the swarm, the tortoise, the volley. The enemy tells you what they hold. You have seconds to answer it.

Lean the right way and their line folds. Lean the wrong way and you watch four thousand men become two.

Press harder to win faster and spend them faster. Wager everything on the shape you already hold. Commit the reserve — you only get to do it once.

Or hand the whole battle to your generals, and live with what they decide.

THREE ANSWER THE CALL. ONE WILL SERVE.

Summon champions out of a thousand years of Vietnamese history. Yết Kiêu the diver. Trần Quốc Toản, too young for the council. Lê Lai, who wore another man's coat and did not come back.

They are not portraits in a menu. They govern your provinces. They lead your armies — and an army with no one at its head fights like it.

They also have lives you did not ask for. Rivals start eating together. A loyal general asks for more than you can give. Someone you paid for years walks out the gate.

THE LEGENDS COME LOOKING FOR YOU

The reed banners. The divine crossbow and the goose feathers. The stakes hidden under the Bạch Đằng. The elders at Diên Hồng who answered with one word.

These are not cutscenes you skip. A story arrives holding the general you actually summoned, and it spends your actual gold and your actual men. Some ask you to swear something — and remember, later, whether you kept it.

DRAWN LIKE A WOODBLOCK PRINT

Every hex, every face, every banner is drawn in the manner of a Đông Hồ print — the folk art of northern Vietnam, cut by hand onto shell-coated paper.

Fifty power cards, each carrying a print of its own: the stakes going into the Bạch Đằng, the Trưng sisters and their elephant, the crossbow on the armourer's bench, the grain fleet at Vân Đồn. Draft them in a run, keep them for good, fold three copies into something stronger.

The paddies flood, ripen and are cut. Winter strips the trees. Your armies stand in the shape they are actually holding, under their own colours, with their camps behind them.

You will be able to read a battle at arm's length.

FOUR WAYS IN

Dragon Ascent — endless waves, cards, and one run that goes as far as you can push it.
Throne of Empires — a reign that outlives you, and a dynasty that inherits it.
Campaign — take the country province by province, by war, marriage or coin.
Skirmish — one fight, nothing else. The quickest way to learn how battles work.

BUY IT ONCE

No ads. No energy timers. No season pass. No premium currency. Nothing waiting behind another payment, ever.

NO ACCOUNT. NO INTERNET NEEDED.

No login, no account, no tracking. The whole game lives on your device — switch off the network on a plane and it plays exactly the same.

Đại Việt is waiting. The drum is falling.

> Ten Thousand Victories is a single-player, paid, fully offline historical strategy game. No account system, no login, no in-app purchase, no subscription, no advertising, no user-generated content. The app requests no permissions of any kind.

1. SCREEN RECORDING
Attached, recorded on a physical device. It shows cold launch, the main menu, starting a Dragon Ascent run, choosing a founding general, the realm screen with the economy running, a battle resolving, and the Chronicle. Airplane Mode is on throughout, demonstrating the app is fully functional with no network.

Nothing else on your list exists in the app to record: there are no registration, login or account-deletion flows because there are no accounts; no purchase or subscription flows because the app is a one-time paid download with nothing further to buy; no user-generated content and therefore no reporting or blocking; and no permission prompts.

2. DEVICES AND OPERATING SYSTEMS TESTED
- iPhone 17, iOS 26 (latest public release), installed from TestFlight

3. FUNCTIONS AND TARGET AUDIENCE
The player rules a small realm in medieval Dai Viet (Vietnam): raising armies, managing four resources, appointing generals drawn from real Vietnamese history, and defending against escalating waves of invasion. Four modes are included - Dragon Ascent (the main mode), Throne of Empires, Campaign and Skirmish.

The audience is strategy players aged 13 and over, particularly Vietnamese players and anyone interested in Vietnamese history. That history is widely taught inside Vietnam and almost unknown outside it, and inside Vietnam is usually met only as textbook material. This app makes it playable, and a built-in History page states what actually happened alongside what the game made of it.

4. SETUP AND ACCESS
No setup, no credentials, no sample files, nothing gated. On first launch the app shows a short optional tour, then the main menu:
- "Rong Thang Long / Dragon Ascent" starts the main mode immediately
- "Classic Modes" opens Throne of Empires, Campaign and Skirmish
- "How to Play" and "History" are reference pages
- The English / Tieng Viet switch is on the main menu

5. EXTERNAL SERVICES
None used for gameplay. No analytics, no advertising SDK, no authentication, no payment processor, no AI service, no third-party data provider. All game logic and content are in the binary and run on the device.

The app serves its own content from an HTTP server bound to 127.0.0.1 inside the app process, which the bundled web view loads. This is entirely on-device, and is why the app works with the network switched off.

The app checks for a content update when it launches. No personal data is transmitted and nothing is collected. The check never blocks startup: the game always launches from the copy already on the device, so it runs identically with no network, in Airplane Mode, for its entire lifetime.

6. REGIONAL DIFFERENCES
None. Features and content are identical in every region. The app ships in English and Vietnamese, both complete, and the language is chosen by the user in the app rather than by region. There is no region-locked content and no server that could vary behaviour.

7. REGULATED INDUSTRY AND THIRD-PARTY MATERIAL
Neither applies.
- Historical figures and events come from the public historical record of Vietnam and from folk legend, and are in the public domain.
- Artwork is of two kinds, both original to this project and both in the manner of Dong Ho folk woodblock printing, a traditional Vietnamese art form. Most of what is on screen - the map, the terrain, the character portraits, the armies in the field - is drawn procedurally by the app's own code at run time. The rest is a library of still illustrations commissioned for this app, produced with an AI image generation tool and reviewed by the developer, used on the power cards and the story screens. No existing woodblock print is reproduced, and no third-party, stock or licensed artwork is used.
- Fonts are Be Vietnam Pro and Source Serif 4, both under the SIL Open Font License.
- No trademarks or licensed properties appear in the app.
