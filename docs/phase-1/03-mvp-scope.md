# MVP Scope

## Included

The first playable MVP includes:

- 1 vertical map
- 15 lands
- 3 kingdoms
- 5 resources
- 6 land types
- 3 unit types
- 12 heroes
- 20 politics cards
- basic bot AI
- tap land selection
- bottom-sheet actions
- tap-to-move army movement
- simple battle resolver
- enemy castle capture win condition

## Land Types

- Castle
- Farm
- Market
- Iron
- Temple
- Enemy Castle

## Hero Types

- General
- Governor
- Minister
- Agent

## Out of Scope For MVP

- save/load
- multiplayer
- family trees
- deep character relationships
- real historical simulation
- naval movement
- advanced diplomacy
- manual building placement
- complex tactical combat

## MVP Success Criteria

The MVP is successful when a player can:

1. Start with one castle and one army.
2. Tap lands to inspect value and available actions.
3. Acquire neutral land by spending resources.
4. Upgrade owned lands.
5. Draft a hero.
6. Move an army through adjacent lands.
7. Resolve a battle against an enemy land.
8. Capture all enemy castles and trigger victory.
