# Content Design

## Starting Scenario

The player begins at `Thăng Long Castle` with one army and nearby opportunities:

- `Thanh Hóa Rice Village`: early food.
- `Nghệ An Iron Mountain`: unlocks heavy infantry pressure.
- `Hội An Market`: stronger gold income.
- `Trúc Lâm Temple`: influence and stability.
- `Bandit Forest`: a first war target.

## Example Lands

### Thanh Hóa Rice Village

- Type: Farm
- Bonus: food
- Defense: low
- Purpose: solves early food pressure

### Nghệ An Iron Mountain

- Type: Iron
- Bonus: gold and heavy infantry access
- Defense: medium
- Purpose: military tech pressure

### Hội An Market

- Type: Market
- Bonus: gold
- Defense: low
- Purpose: economy-heavy play

### Trúc Lâm Temple

- Type: Temple
- Bonus: influence and stability
- Defense: low
- Purpose: better politics and peaceful expansion

## Hero Examples

- `Võ Tướng Núi`: General, strong in mountain and iron lands.
- `Quan Đốc Lương`: Governor, improves farm output.
- `Sứ Giả Mềm Mỏng`: Agent, improves peaceful acquisition.
- `Quan Thuế Hội An`: Minister, improves market income.

## Politics Card Examples

Politics cards should offer two clear choices.

### Flood in River Village

- Send Relief: spend gold, gain stability and loyalty.
- Ignore: save gold, lose food and stability.

### Noble Estate Demands Autonomy

- Accept: gain loyalty, lose gold income.
- Reject: gain short-term gold, lose stability.

### Bandit Prisoners Captured

- Execute: gain stability, lose influence.
- Recruit Them: gain manpower, lose stability.

## Unit Design

MVP units:

- Spearmen: cheap baseline.
- Archers: efficient defense and forest support.
- Heavy Infantry: unlocked by iron lands and strong in assaults.
